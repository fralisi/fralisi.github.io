What's your obstacle at work?

Most people I work with don't lack talent or ambition – something specific is standing in their way. Does any of this sound familiar?

  • “I feel stuck in my role.”You are doing good work, but your career isn't moving forward.
  • “I keep getting overlooked.”Your impact isn't visible to the people who make promotion decisions.
  • “I'm not hearing back.”Your applications and CV aren't turning into interviews.
  • “I get interviews, but no offers.”You are close, but something in the interview isn't landing.
  • “I don't know what's next.”You are ready for a change but unsure which direction to take.
  • “I've lost my confidence.”Self-doubt is stopping you from going after what you want.

Whatever your obstacle, that's where we start.

Together we name what is really holding you back, understand why, and build a practical plan to move past it.

How do I book a session?

Fill in the 2-minute form above, or email me directly. I read every request and will get back to you to arrange a discovery session at a time that suits you.

Because that is where real progress starts. Knowing what is holding you back before we meet means our discovery session can go straight to what matters most to you.

Yes. Sessions take place online by video call, so you can join from wherever you are.

How much does coaching cost?

Pricing depends on the type and amount of support you need. We will agree a tailored package after the discovery session.

I'm not targeting consulting or Big Tech – can you still help?

Absolutely. Premium Interview Preparation focuses equally on MBB consulting and Big Tech, while job search strategy, CV reviews and career planning work across industries and career stages.
