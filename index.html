FAQ

Good to know.

What's the investment?

It depends on the support you need – a single session, an interview preparation package or a longer programme. We discuss it on the free 15-minute intro call, once we both know it's a good fit. There's no commitment.

Can you guarantee I'll get the job?

No – and no honest coach can. Results depend on your background, your timing and the effort you put in. What I can promise is honest feedback, a clear plan and my full commitment to helping you give your best.

I've already been rejected by a top firm – is it too late?

Not at all. A rejection shows you what to work on, and most firms let you reapply after a waiting period. We look honestly at what didn't land – your story, your cases or your behavioural answers – and build a plan so you're better prepared next time.

My background isn't classic consulting or tech – can I compete?

Yes. My own path went from consulting to Big Tech, so I know how to translate experience from one world to another. What matters is how you connect your background to the role: together we find your strengths and turn them into a story recruiters understand.

I'm not targeting consulting or Big Tech – can you still help?

Absolutely. Interview preparation focuses on MBB consulting and Big Tech, but job search strategy, CV reviews and career planning work across industries and career stages.
